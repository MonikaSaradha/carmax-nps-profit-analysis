# CarMax NPS & Profitability Analysis

## Overview

This project presents a business analytics study conducted to analyze the relationship between Net Promoter Score (NPS), customer satisfaction, and profitability at CarMax.

The objective is to identify key drivers of customer experience and recommend strategies to improve both customer satisfaction and financial performance.

---

## Tools Used

* KNIME (primary tool for data analysis and workflow design)
* Excel (data handling and preparation)

---

## Problem Statement

CarMax experienced a significant decline in financial performance, with a **31.1% decrease in gross profit** over a recent quarter.

The goal of this analysis was to understand how improving customer satisfaction (measured using NPS) could impact revenue and profitability.

---

## Key Insights

* A strong relationship exists between **NPS and profitability**
* A **2% increase in NPS can lead to approximately $16 million in additional profit**
* Customer trust increased by **12%**, contributing to improved financial outcomes
* Combined strategic initiatives could result in:

  * **$650 million increase in revenue**
  * **$52 million increase in profit**

---

## Analysis Performed

* Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis
* Financial performance evaluation
* Customer behavior insights
* Identification of key drivers affecting satisfaction
* Strategy impact estimation

---

## Strategic Recommendations

The following initiatives were proposed to improve NPS and financial performance:

* Improve customer service and response time
* Enhance issue resolution processes
* Increase employee engagement
* Leverage customer feedback systems
* Optimize pricing strategies
* Improve online and in-store customer experience

---

## Business Impact

Implementing the recommended strategies can:

* Increase customer satisfaction and loyalty
* Improve revenue and profitability
* Strengthen brand trust and long-term growth

---

## Data Availability

The dataset used in this project is not publicly available due to confidentiality and usage restrictions.

However, the methodology, insights, and results are fully documented in the report and presentation provided.

---

## Reproducibility

This project was implemented using KNIME workflows.

Due to the unavailability of the dataset, the analysis cannot be directly reproduced. However, the methodology can be applied to similar datasets.
